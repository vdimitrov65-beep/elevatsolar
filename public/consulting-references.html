Consulting References

Elevat Solar Consulting OOD provides independent advisory services for photovoltaic, battery storage and hybrid projects in Bulgaria and Southeast Europe. Below are the firm's service lines and anonymized references from completed engagements.

Service Lines

Technical Due Diligence Independent technical review of PV assets for acquisition or financing: design documentation review, equipment assessment, yield analysis, regulatory compliance check.
Owner's Engineering Investor representation during construction of utility-scale PV: EPC performance oversight, quality control, acceptance testing, coordination through commissioning.
BESS Feasibility Assessment of technical and economic viability for retrofitting battery storage onto operating PV plants: sizing, revenue-stacking models, grid code compliance.
Preliminary Feasibility Pre-investment technical and economic analysis for new PV and BESS projects, including cashback modelling and investment attractiveness assessment before engaging a detailed designer.
Grid Connection Strategy Connection strategy under Ordinance No 6 of 28.03.2024: capacity analysis, application preparation, coordination with the DSO/TSO, support through contract execution.
Permitting Navigating the Bulgarian construction permitting regime under the Spatial Planning Act (ZUT) for PV and BESS projects: from zoning plan through construction permit to commissioning permit.
Conceptual Design Feasibility-level conceptual solutions: virtual battery hub architectures, building-integrated PV facade systems, hybridization of existing PV parks with wind and BESS.
