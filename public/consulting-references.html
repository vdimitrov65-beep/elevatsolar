Independent Technical and Investment Advisory for PV, BESS and Hybrid Projects

Elevat Solar Consulting is the advisory practice of Elevat Solar OOD, providing independent technical and investment advisory for solar PV plants, battery storage and hybrid energy projects across Southeast Europe.

Elevat Solar Consulting · Southeast Europe · Project assessment and advisory experience across more than 400 MWp

Elevat Solar Consulting works on the owner side and on the investor side. Technical decisions, market scenarios and investment conclusions rest on our own analysis. Potential conflicts of interest arising from the firm's EPC activity are disclosed and managed at engagement level.

Our role

Elevat engages with projects in two roles.

Owner-side technical and investment advisory. On the owner's or the buyer's side, in acquisition, development or optimisation of an existing asset. We protect investor interests in decisions where the EPC contractor has a counter interest.

Investor-side technical and economic analysis. On the side of an investor or a fund, in the assessment of an opportunity before capital is committed. We deliver a structured review fit for a decision on whether to deepen due diligence.

Services

Preparation of technical and commercial offers for utility-scale PV, BESS and hybrid projects

Scope structuring, quantity take-off and pricing for large investment projects. Applied in tender submissions, in negotiations with EPC contractors, and in forming an internal investment view.

Optimal technical and commercial configurations for hybrid projects combining PV, wind and BESS

Component sizing and ratios, dispatch logic, market mode, coordination between renewable sources and storage. Applied in new hybrid project planning and in hybridisation of existing assets.

Owner's engineer and independent technical advisor on acquisition and development

Design validation, coordination with the EPC contractor, independent progress and acceptance control, protection of investor interests. End-to-end support across the project life cycle.

Technical due diligence

Independent assessment of operating assets and development pipelines for buyers, lenders and investors. Covers design and construction documentation, performance, technical condition, grid integration, regulatory compliance and residual risk.

Grid connection advisory

Connection to transmission and distribution networks in Bulgaria under Ordinance No. 6 on the connection of producers and clients to the electricity networks, in force from 14.04.2026, together with the Spatial Planning Act and the Energy Act, and in Romania. Procedure selection, document package preparation, work with the transmission or distribution operator, timeline management.

Procurement advisory

Independent evaluation and selection of suppliers and equipment for modules, inverters, storage systems, mounting structures and EPC contractors. We maintain our own file of more than 27 suppliers, actively refreshed for pricing and technical benchmarking.

Feasibility and concept for hybridisation of amortised PV plants with wind and BESS

A specialised application for owners of PV plants built between 2012 and 2016 whose feed-in tariff is expiring. We examine technical feasibility, permit changes, economic effect and the priority order of implementation.

Our own analytical and conceptual work

Alongside client engagements, the firm develops its own theoretical and applied analyses. They are not client mandates. They are the groundwork on which later client decisions are built.

Hybridisation of amortised PV plants with wind and BESS

I am inclined to think that hybridising PV plants built in Bulgaria between 2012 and 2016, with low residual CAPEX and an existing grid position, is more economically attractive than greenfield development in the current market environment of Southeast Europe.
