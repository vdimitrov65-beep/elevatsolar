700 kW PV + 700 kWh BESS at an industrial site in Western Bulgaria

A 700 kW photovoltaic installation combined with a 700 kWh battery energy storage system at an industrial production site in Western Bulgaria. The BESS is configured for the owner's self-consumption during peak-price hours. Delivered by Elevat Solar OOD as EPC contractor. Commissioning period: 2023-2024.

Scope delivered

Elevat Solar OOD acted as EPC contractor across the full engineering, procurement and construction scope for both the PV and the BESS parts, integrated with the owner's existing on-site electrical infrastructure:

  • Detailed electrical design for PV and BESS integration with the industrial site's internal grid
  • Equipment procurement for modules, inverters, mounting, battery system and controls
  • Installation on site, coordinated with ongoing production operations
  • DC and AC cabling, protection and metering
  • EMS configuration for load-following, peak-shaving and self-consumption during high-price hours
  • Grid connection coordination with the DSO
  • Commissioning and acceptance testing
  • Handover with as-built documentation and O&M manual

Why this configuration

The host facility runs a continuous production process with substantial baseload electricity demand. The 700 kW PV covers a share of the daytime load; the 700 kWh BESS captures surplus daytime generation and discharges during the morning and evening high-price hours, reducing the owner's exposure to peak tariffs.
