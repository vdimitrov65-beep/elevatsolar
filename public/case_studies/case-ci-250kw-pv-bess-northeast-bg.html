EPC delivery, PV + BESS

250 kW PV + 250 kWh BESS at a commercial site in Northeastern Bulgaria

A 250 kW photovoltaic installation combined with a 250 kWh battery energy storage system at a commercial site in Northeastern Bulgaria, delivered by Elevat Solar OOD as EPC contractor. Commissioning period: 2023-2024.

Scope delivered

Elevat Solar OOD acted as EPC contractor across the full engineering, procurement and construction scope for both the PV and the BESS parts:

  • Detailed electrical design for PV and BESS integration at a shared point of connection
  • Equipment procurement for modules, inverters, mounting, battery system and controls
  • Installation on site
  • DC and AC cabling, protection and metering
  • EMS configuration for coordinated PV and BESS operation
  • Grid connection coordination with the DSO
  • Commissioning and acceptance testing
  • Handover with as-built documentation and O&M manual
