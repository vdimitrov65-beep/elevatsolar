Portfolio

Elevat Solar OOD operates in two complementary service areas on PV, BESS and hybrid projects: EPC delivery on installation engagements in Bulgaria, and independent technical and investment advisory for owners, investors and developers across Southeast Europe. Both activities are carried out by the same legal entity. The advisory practice uses the Elevat Solar Consulting trading name. Entries below are grouped by the actual scope of services performed.

EPC delivery

Role. EPC contractor on PV and PV plus BESS installations in Bulgaria. Engineering, procurement and construction delivered under one contract, with grid connection coordination, commissioning and handover with as-built documentation.
